Attach music to a post. The phone shows it: the track name scrolling along the bottom, the artist
credited, the audio page one tap away. Open that identical post in a desktop browser and the music
is **absent from the description of the post** — not muted, not broken, just never mentioned. The
attachment you deliberately made is invisible on the surface where you might actually be cataloguing
your own work.

Which leaves you in the position of not being able to confirm, from your computer, whether the thing
you attached is attached. The only way to check what your post contains is to pick up a different
device.

## The same root as a real case

This is the quiet version of [case 032](/cases/032-no-next-reel-on-desktop/): Instagram's desktop
client isn't a client, it's a **partial rendering** of one. Case 032 is the part that costs
measurable time — no next reel, so you go back and click. This one costs almost none, and is
arguably worse, because it doesn't waste your time so much as **misinform you about your own
content**. A missing button is visible. Missing metadata looks exactly like metadata that was never
there.

Two surfaces, one database, and no shared definition of what a post *is*.
