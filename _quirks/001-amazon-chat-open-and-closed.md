The customer-service page offers **Chat with us**, stamps it 🕐 **Available now** in reassuring
green, highlights a **Start chatting now** button, even labels the whole card **Recommended** — and,
in a box directly to the right, announces: *"We are currently closed for the day. The normal
business hours are from 06:00 to 00:00."* There's no lifetime math to run here: you lose a few
seconds and a little faith. But it's a perfect specimen of the genre — two components each shipped
a true sentence, and nobody read the page as a whole. The chat widget knows the bots are awake. The
hours module knows the humans aren't. The page is where they were supposed to meet, and didn't.

## Why quirks exist

Some things aren't worth a napkin. A contradiction like this wastes seconds, not lifetimes — but it
reveals the same disease the big cases do: **nobody looked at the screen the way a human would.**
Quirks are the field notes. Cases are the invoices.
